Vow & Vine is the event-services arm of the Jack & Jill family, built for hosts who want to be guests at their own celebrations.

Our Story

Your day, fully present

Vow & Vine started with a question every host eventually asks: who takes care of everything so I can actually enjoy my own event? We do.

From weddings and birthdays to office parties, we plan the details, set the scene, staff the day, and break it all down at the end. When something extra is needed, we bring in our network of trusted local vendors, including catering from our sister brand, The Gathered Table.

Family owned, locally rooted, and proud to be part of Oregon and Washington's award-winning Jack & Jill family.
